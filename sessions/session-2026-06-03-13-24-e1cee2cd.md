## The three ideas it teaches

1. **The browser is never trusted.** It can *start* a payment, but only a signature-verified **webhook from Razorpay's servers** marks a fee truly `paid`. You'll watch the status climb `created → attempted → client_verified → paid` — that last hop is the webhook landing.

2. **The four IDs and who to contact** (`docs/02`): your `our_ref`, Razorpay's `order_id`, Razorpay's `payment_id` (→ give to **Razorpay** support), and the bank's `RRN`/`ARN` (→ give to the **bank**). Plus a failure-mode table: "money debited but we show unpaid," lost webhooks, bad signatures — what each means and what to do.

3. **Security mechanics** (`docs/03`): the two HMAC signature checks (checkout callback vs webhook), why webhooks must verify the *raw bytes*, constant-time comparison, secrets that never reach the browser, idempotency on retries, and money as integer paise (never floats).

## To run it (needs your Razorpay test keys + ngrok)
The `README.md` has the full 3-terminal walkthrough. The short version:
