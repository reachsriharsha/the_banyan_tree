### One-paragraph verdict

Diamond Power Infrastructure has executed a genuine post-NCLT turnaround. From a near-dead asset in 2022 (admitted claims ₹3,308 cr, ~85% haircut), it has rebuilt to a ₹1,620 cr (9M FY26 annualised) revenue run-rate at 12-14% EBITDA margins, a ₹3,500 cr+ order book, and a credible Adani Group anchor. The plant, capex pipeline (4 rod mills, 10 MV lines, 5th CCV line, EHV up to 400 kV) and team look real. **However**, three issues keep this in the WATCH bucket rather than BUY-WORTHY: (1) tangible net worth is still negative ₹881 cr, (2) the credit rating is still "BWR D / Issuer Not Cooperating" — the company hasn't engaged with Brickwork since 2022, and (3) Adani Group and Aditya Birla / UltraTech have publicly announced their own cable manufacturing in 2026, which directly threatens the ~70% of order book that is Adani-linked. At CMP ₹158 (~₹8,320 cr mcap, ~64× annualised PAT), valuation already prices in 2-3 years of perfect execution.
