## What must actually change before real money

The ngrok URL is the obvious one — Razorpay requires a real HTTPS endpoint for live webhooks; a laptop tunnel won't do. Beyond that, this sandbox is deliberately a teaching toy and its own `docs/03-security.md §6` lists what it leaves out. The ones that bite hardest with real money:

1. **A real database with idempotency** (`§4`). Razorpay retries a webhook until it gets a 200, and parents double-click. Right now the store is an in-memory dict — restart the server and every payment record is gone. Live needs a DB row with a **unique constraint on `payment_id`** and "only move status forward" so a duplicate `payment.captured` can never double-count.

2. **Don't trust the amount from the browser** (`§1`). Today the amount comes from the form. In live, the amount must come from the **student's fee record on the server** — otherwise anyone can open dev tools and pay ₹1 for a ₹50,000 fee.

3. **Auth** — who is allowed to pay which student's fee. The sandbox has none.

4. **Daily reconciliation job** (`§5`). Webhooks *do* occasionally get lost. A nightly job that fetches Razorpay's settlement report and compares it to your DB is what guarantees a paid fee never silently stays "unpaid."

5. **Refunds** — the `refund.*` webhook events aren't handled here at all.

The signature verification, the "browser is never trusted / webhook is the truth" principle, the Decimal-paise money handling, and constant-time compares — those are all already correct in this sandbox and carry straight over to production unchanged.
