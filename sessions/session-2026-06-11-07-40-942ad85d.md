**The four things UPI adds that you'll actually learn (all surfaced in the UI and audit trail):**
1. **VPA** — the payer's UPI identity (`name@bank`), a first-class ID you legitimately see and store.
2. **A real `pending` state** — a UPI collect *waits* for the payer to approve in their app, so the status strip now reads `created → attempted → pending → paid`. The card version jumps straight to paid; watching it dwell in pending is the lesson.
3. **UTR** — UPI's bank reference (what the payer quotes to *their* bank), captured from the webhook/reconcile.
4. **collect vs intent/QR** — two flows cards don't have. I wired the **collect** flow two ways: via Checkout (always works) and via an optional S2S endpoint (flagged as needing account enablement; it shows a friendly explanation if your account lacks it).
